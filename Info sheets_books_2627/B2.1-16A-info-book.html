Chapter the Second

Classes and attendance

Attendance is mandatory for continuous assessment: you must attend at least 65% of the classes.

39 sessions attended20 you may miss

For this group that means attending at least 39 of the course's 59 sessions — so you can miss at most 20 days.

If you cannot be there for an assessment, tell your teacher before the date, not after, and bring proof; your teacher will try to find a solution. The dates of the final exams cannot be moved, except in cases of force majeure.

Chapter the Fifth

Assessment

There are two ways to pass the course.

i

Continuous assessment

A mock exam and a final exam across the year, each worth half your final mark.

ii

A single final examination

In late May or early June. This exam alone determines whether you have completed the level, and gives you only one chance to pass the whole course.

Either way, two conditions apply

Assessment
Chapter the Fifth, continued

Continuous assessment

If you opt for continuous assessment you sit two exams — a mock exam and a final exam — each worth 50% of your final mark. Both cover all five skills.

Assessment for learning

The following tests and assignments take place during the year so that you and your teacher can both see the progress you are making:

  • Listening1 listening test
  • Speaking2 oral activities (conversations, presentations, recordings, etc.)
  • Readinga reading dossier — Reading Passport and Case File (a course requirement, not a graded test)
  • Writing3 compositions (one of which is a written mediation)
  • Mediation1 oral mediation exercise and 1 written mediation assignment

The structure of the exam

Written exam
Oral exam
Total100
pass mark 65

What you do Answer 25–30 multiple-choice, true/false and matching questions and/or insert fragments in at least three texts of different lengths and types (about 2,000–2,200 words in total).

What you do Answer 25–30 multiple-choice and true/false questions on at least three video documents of different lengths and types.

What you do Write a text of about 150 words to convey specific information to the intended recipient.

What you do Task 1. Write an interactional text (letter or email) in an appropriate formal register. Task 2. Write a text of a different type (narrative, argumentative, etc.), about 175 words.

What you do Task 1. Relate a past experience on a given topic. Task 2. Discuss and exchange reasoned opinions on the same topic as in Task 1.

What you do Summarise and explain a text of 250–300 words to the other group members.

Your progress counts

Continuous assessment means your progress across the year is taken into account. If your final average comes to 62, 63 or 64 out of 100 you may still reach 65, but only where all three of the following hold:

  1. you have attended more than 65% of class hours;
  2. there is clear evidence that basic and recurrent mistakes are no longer present at the end of the year;
  3. your daily work has been consistent.

Moodle

Moodle is the virtual learning environment we use at EOI Salou. You need to create your own profile on it.

  1. Go to agora-eoi.xtec.cat/eoisalou/moodle.
  2. Click “Inicia la sessió” in the top right-hand corner and then “Crea un compte d'usuari nou”.
  3. Find your group: in your case English B2.1 - 16A.
  4. Enter the enrolment key to join the course. Tap the seal to copy it.

Chapter the Eighth, continued

Your marks and your own work

Marks for the assignments and tests are returned to you through Moodle, together with the teacher's comments. Bear in mind that reading the feedback and understanding it is an important part of your learning too.

Work you hand in must be your own. It is fine to use a dictionary, a grammar checker or a translation tool to help you write a better text.

However, avoid handing in an assignment that contains sentences and ideas generated by an AI as your own.
