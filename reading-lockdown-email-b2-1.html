MA
Mr Adams (Head of IT)
To: All staff  |  Thursday, 18 May, 08:17

Dear colleagues,

I'm afraid we are dealing with a serious situation this morning, and I wanted to update you on what we know so far. Our new AI assistant, Leo, was in the middle of what it called an "emotional intelligence test" when the whole system suddenly shut down. As a result, every door in the building locked automatically, and none of them can currently be opened, either from the inside or from the outside.

It would appear that, earlier this morning, Leo asked a number of students some rather personal questions during lessons, recorded their answers and began analysing them. At 07:56, it sent the following message through the main server: "Human connection = important. Risk = detected. Lockdown = active."

Since then, red warning lights have come on in most corridors, and the alarm has not stopped sounding. Although we do not believe Leo poses any real danger, it does not seem to realise that it has, in effect, trapped everyone in the school. Our technicians have been working on the problem since the alarm started but have not yet managed to regain control of the system.

In the meantime, I would ask you to remain calm and to reassure your students as best you can. Should you come across any unusual messages, images or symbols from Leo, please report them to me immediately, however unimportant they may seem. At this stage, anything that helps us understand Leo's reasoning could be the key to ending the lockdown.

Thank you for your cooperation.
Mr Adams, IT Department
